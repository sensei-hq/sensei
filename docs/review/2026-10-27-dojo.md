# dōjō functional review — 2026-10-27

An adversarial, read-only walk through every screen of the dōjō web app (`dojo/`), written for
going through screen by screen. Nothing in code, config, the database or git was changed.

## How this was reviewed

- **Code:** all 16 `+page.svelte` files under `dojo/src/routes`, their loads, the `Scr*` screen
  components, `lib/*-data.ts` / `lib/server/*`, the `/v1` routes the daemon calls, the daemon's
  `crates/senseid/src/tasks/dojo_sync.rs`, the policies in `database/policies/dojo/` and the
  definer functions in `database/ddl/function/dojo/`.
- **Local dev server** (`localhost:5173`, GET only): it is up, and every guarded page redirects
  to `/signin` without a session (`/you` → 303, `/org/x/members` → 303, `/v1/you/workspaces` → 401,
  `/version` → 200). Nothing past sign-in was exercised in the browser, so screen behaviour below
  comes from the code.
- **Local DB** (`127.0.0.1:54322`, SELECT only): 14 tenants (4 organization, 10 personal),
  37 workspaces, 57 repositories, 7,088 metric rows. **All 14 memberships are `admin`**, and
  there are 0 teams, viewers and seats. So the contributor, maintainer, lead and viewer cells
  **cannot be tried locally without seeding**; only the SQL tests cover them. One tenant,
  `organization/global-dojo`, has no membership and no admin. 20 repositories are in no
  workspace. The local `dojo.principals` ids and `auth.users` ids never overlap (12 and 19 rows,
  0 shared), which matters for finding R13-3.
- **Mockups:** none of the PNGs asked for shows a dōjō screen. `docs/mockups/Sensei/hq/*.png` is
  the marketing site (`_dojo-teams*.png`, the "For teams · Dōjō" landing) plus Observatory
  desktop-app slides (`_sidebar`, `_subtabs*`, `_prefs`, `_boot-*`).
  `docs/mockups/Sensei/assets/screens/*.png` is the desktop app (Today, Projects, Sessions…). The
  dōjō's real mockup is the JSX in `docs/mockups/Sensei/lib/dojo2/dojo2-app.jsx` (with
  `dojo2-viewer.jsx` and `dojo2-project.jsx`); the comparisons below use it, and say "no PNG
  mockup" where that is all there is.
- Decisions D1–D39 come from `docs/roadmap/09-dojo-expansion.md`; the access matrix from
  `docs/spec/dojo/access-matrix.md`.

Severity: **bug** (wrong behaviour or data) · **gap** (missing against spec, decision or mockup)
· **polish** · **question** (needs your call).

**Roles.** The ladder is `contributor < maintainer < lead < admin` (D1); "developer" means
contributor. A **viewer** is not a member (D8). The org nav shows Overview to everyone, Govern
from maintainer and Admin from admin (`dojo/src/lib/nav.ts:61-95`). The nav filter is only
display: each section's real gate is the role floor of the `/v1/t/...` route it calls.

## Cross-cutting findings (these affect many screens)

- **X1 · The org home throws away its workspaces error — bug.** `/org/[slug]/+page.ts:29-35`
  computes `workspacesError`, but `+page.svelte:22-29` never passes it to `ScrOrgHome`, and
  `ScrOrgHome` has no prop for it. A failed read therefore renders *"No workspaces in this
  jurisdiction yet … Nothing is hidden — this is everything the dōjō governs."*
  (`ScrOrgHome.svelte:91-95`). This breaks the never-fabricate rule on the first screen of every
  dōjō. It is the same symptom as the production "no workspaces" report, and it hides the cause.
- **X2 · Org workspaces need lead, but the page and the matrix say otherwise — bug.**
  `GET /v1/t/.../workspaces` takes `ACCESS.lead`
  (`dojo/src/routes/v1/t/[origin]/[org]/workspaces/+server.ts:13`). Its comment at :3 still says
  "their teams'", which D7 retired. The matrix gives the workspace read to anyone with a role on
  one of its repositories. As a result:
  - every contributor and maintainer gets a 403 on the org home, which X1 turns into "No
    workspaces";
  - Overview › Workspaces (shown to everyone) shows a load error;
  - the workspace drill-in silently bounces (X4);
  - yet `/you/workspaces` lists the same workspaces through RLS.
- **X3 · Many controls do nothing — gap.** Buttons render with no handler and give no feedback
  when clicked. The matrix, or a `/v1` route, often already supports the action:
  - **Triage:** Approve, Revise, Decline (`ScrTriage.svelte:193-196`) and My scopes (:63).
    `decideTriage` exists client-side (`lib/triage-data.ts:125`) and the route exists, but
    nothing calls it.
  - **Approvals:** Review and Approve — the page never passes `onReview`/`onApprove`
    (`org/[slug]/[section]/+page.svelte:154`).
  - **Knowledge:** Edit and Keep (`ScrKnowledge.svelte:71,88`).
  - **Identity:** Configure, Manage, Edit, Add mapping (`ScrIdentity.svelte:60,76,83,92`).
  - **Scopes:** New scope, Assign (`ScrScopes.svelte:52,101`).
  - **Role surfaces:** "Edit policy" (`ScrRoleSurfaces.svelte:233`), and the header CTA on the
    Policies tab ("New policy") and the Audit tab ("Export") (`role-surfaces-view.ts:64,73`,
    `cta` is `undefined` at `ScrRoleSurfaces.svelte:98-105`).
  - **Org Constitution:** "Adopt pack" (`ScrOrgLadder.svelte:130`).
  - **Elsewhere:** Org home "By team" (`ScrOrgHome.svelte:86`), Workspaces "Filter"
    (`ScrWorkspaces.svelte:36`), Rule packs "Browse all" (`ScrRulePacks.svelte:64`), and the
    self-host "Connect" on sign-in (`DojoSignIn.svelte:171-175`).
- **X4 · Drill-ins turn read errors into redirects — gap.** `/you/workspaces/[id]/+page.ts:26-28`
  and `/org/[slug]/workspaces/[id]/+page.ts:28-30` `catch { redirect(...) }`. A failure looks
  exactly like "that workspace doesn't exist", and the user lands back on the list with no
  message.
- **X5 · Nothing on the dōjō side shows sync health — gap.** This is why production showed an
  empty dōjō with no hint of the cause. A Keychain or token failure in the daemon makes no HTTP
  call at all (`crates/senseid/src/tasks/dojo_sync.rs:198-205`). The dōjō keeps no heartbeat or
  last-registration record; the only timestamp is `last_synced_at` = max metric `pushed_at`
  (`database/ddl/.../all_my_repositories.ddl:157`). Three cases render the same "No workspaces
  yet": "never synced", "sync failing since 10-22", and "synced, zero repositories" (the daemon
  returns before registering at `dojo_sync.rs:409`). On the daemon side a token failure never
  writes `sync_state`, so the desktop Settings › dōjō row stays green "synced" (bug,
  `dojo_sync.rs:198,205` vs `dojo-status-state.svelte.ts:38-41`, contradicting
  `daemon-sync.md:123`).
- **X6 · No guard against removing the last admin — bug.**
  - `setMemberRole` (`dojo/src/lib/server/admin-data.ts:289-308`) and the `memberships` admin
    policy allow an admin to demote anyone, including themselves and the last admin.
  - The UI meant to protect the admin's own row (`ScrRoleSurfaces.svelte:170`
    `!m.you`) never fires: `toKitMembers` compares `m.user_id` (a **principal** id) with
    `user.id` (the **Supabase auth** id) (`org/[slug]/[section]/+page.ts:169`,
    `console-load.ts:65`, `admin-map.ts:72`). Those ids never match (local DB: 0 overlap).
  - So an admin sees a role picker on their own row, can demote themselves, and can leave the
    dōjō with no admin. Locally, `organization/global-dojo` already has none.
- **X7 · The fixture tenant fallback is still live — question.**
  - `DEFAULT_TENANT_KEY = orgs[0]?.url ?? 'github/acme'` comes from the `dojo-data` fixture
    (`lib/tenant.ts:37`). It is returned when a member has no cookie and no `memberDefault` is
    passed (`tenant.ts:80`).
  - The cookie and `?tenant=` "always win, membership or not" (`tenant.ts:62-63`). A stale cookie
    for a dōjō you have left is therefore still used as `tenantKey` by `/you/runs/[id]`.
  - Check whether `deriveConsoleContext` always passes the real first membership. If it does not,
    a member can be pointed at `github/acme`.
- **X8 · The "Needs" bell is hard-wired to 0** (`(app)/+layout.svelte:76`), and the comment says
  so. On mobile the "Needs" tab always shows nothing — **gap**.

## Public and auth routes

### `/` — root redirect (`routes/+page.ts`, `+page.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** the OAuth return target. It sends a session to `/you` and anyone else to `/signin`
  (307).
- **Data:** only `session` from the root layout. No DB call.
- **Mockup:** no PNG mockup.
- **Findings:** the fallback text says "Redirecting to sign in…" even when it is sending you to
  `/you` (`+page.svelte:7`) — polish.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Signed out, open `/` — expect `/signin`.
  - [ ] Signed in, open `/` — expect `/you`. The code matches.

### `/signin` (`routes/signin/+page.ts`, `DojoSignIn.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** the sign-in options are GitHub (scopes `read:org user:email repo`), Microsoft
  (`email profile`), magic link and password (`kavach.config.js:51-66`). With a session the page
  forwards to `/you` (`signin/+page.ts:13-16`).
- **Data:** no DB read. Provisioning happens in `hooks.server.ts:22` through `onSessionSync`.
- **Interactions:** OAuth and OTP go through `@kavach/ui` `AuthProvider`. Errors from the URL hash
  or query are shown (`DojoSignIn.svelte:22-34`).
- **Mockup:** `ScrSignIn` in `dojo2-app.jsx:1321`. The screens spec already records that the left
  panel diverges.
- **Findings:**
  - **gap:** the self-hosted block contradicts **D17/D24** ("no self-hosting product"). The
    `Connect` button has no handler, and the URL field is pre-filled with the fixture
    `dojo.acme.internal` (`DojoSignIn.svelte:9,145-180`). The footer still says "One sign-in for
    the hosted SaaS and any self-hosted Dōjō" (:186).
  - **question:** the GitHub `repo` scope is full read+write over all private code, and the CLI
    path keeps that token in the Keychain (`kavach.config.js:32-50`). The config itself flags the
    GitHub App migration.
  - **question:** if provisioning fails in `onSessionSync`, does the user see anything, or only an
    empty "My dōjōs"?
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Signed out, click "Connecting to a self-hosted Dōjō?" — expect it gone (D17). Today it
    opens a box pre-filled with `dojo.acme.internal`, and Connect does nothing.
  - [ ] Sign in with GitHub — expect to land on `/you` with your GitHub orgs as dōjōs.
  - [ ] Sign in with a magic link (Inbucket locally) — expect `/you`. A brand-new person should
    see the empty Inbox, not an error.
  - [ ] Cancel the GitHub consent — expect a red message on `/signin`.

### `/logout` (`routes/logout/+page.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** polls for up to 2 s for the browser kavach instance, calls `signOut`, then always
  goes to `/signin` (`:15-28`).
- **Findings:**
  - **polish:** if `signOut` never appears within 2 s, the `finally` still navigates. You reach
    `/signin` with the session cookie possibly intact, and `/signin` then bounces you back to
    `/you`.
  - **question:** the `dojo_tenant` cookie is not cleared on logout. The next person on the same
    browser inherits the selection (see X7).
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Account menu › Log out — expect `/signin`, and `/you` should then redirect to `/signin`.
  - [ ] After logging out, check that the `dojo_tenant` cookie is gone. The code suggests it
    stays.

### `/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-service`
- **Purpose:** static `LegalPage` pages, public (`kavach.config.js:101-102`). No data.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Signed out, open both — expect them to render without a redirect.

### `/orgs` — org picker (`routes/orgs/+page.server.ts`, `DojoOrgs.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** lists your active memberships. "Enter" sets the `dojo_tenant` cookie and goes to
  `/org/{id}`.
- **Data:** `memberships` joined to `tenants` through the person's JWT (`sessionDb`, RLS). The
  principal is looked up with the service role (`dojo-orgs.ts:67-71`, read-only). A failure is a
  500, not an empty list (`dojo-orgs.ts:145`). Correct.
- **Interactions:** `enterOrg` writes a non-HttpOnly cookie (`tenant.ts:105-107`).
- **Findings:**
  - **polish:** this page sits outside the `(app)` shell, while the shell's switcher does the same
    job. Two pickers for one choice.
  - **gap:** member, workspace and pending counts are left `undefined` on purpose
    (`dojo-orgs.ts:103-107`), so rows show no counts.
  - **polish:** copy at `DojoOrgs.svelte:154` says "Roles are derived from your GitHub org & repo
    access". That is half true since D5: the role is mirrored per repository, and the admin may
    override it.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a member of 2+ dōjōs, open `/orgs` — expect both. Click Enter on the second — expect
    `/org/{id}` for that dōjō.
  - [ ] As a person with no membership — expect an empty list, not an error.

## Personal zone (`/you/...`)

The `(app)` layout (`routes/(app)/+layout.server.ts` → `console-load.ts`) resolves the principal,
the memberships (as the person) and the chrome org on every navigation. Errors are 500s, not
empty, which is correct.

### `/you` — Inbox (`you/(inbox)/+layout.ts`, `+layout.svelte`, `+page.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** a two-pane relay inbox across every dōjō you belong to: a list of runs on the left
  and the selected run on the right. The first run opens automatically on md+.
- **Data:** `loadRelayInbox` fans out over your memberships. For each dōjō it calls
  `listRuns` + `listGates`, then `getSegments` once per run (`lib/relay/relay-inbox.ts:30-70`).
  An unreachable dōjō is reported by name, not blanked (`RelayList.svelte:64-68`), and a missing
  plan is counted in `plansMissing`. This is good failure handling.
- **Role gating:** relay content is owner-only (#249). The routes use `asOwner` and the
  contributor floor. Matches the matrix.
- **Interactions:** answering a gate goes to `POST .../relay/reply` in the run's own dōjō
  (`+layout.svelte:39-40`), which is owner-gated server-side.
- **Mockup:** `ScrInbox` (`dojo2-app.jsx:350`). The structure matches.
- **Findings:**
  - **polish/perf:** N+1 — one `getSegments` per run per dōjō on every Inbox load.
  - **gap:** a dōjō viewer has no memberships, so the Inbox is just empty, with no hint that
    viewers belong under Workspaces. The viewer shell `/v/{tenant}` is still to be built (D15).
  - **gap:** push and relay are not yet confirmed working in production (D27).
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a contributor with a run in flight, open `/you` — expect it listed and auto-opened.
  - [ ] Make one dōjō's relay fail (for example a bad key) — expect a "could not reach X" note,
    not an empty list.
  - [ ] Answer a pending gate — expect it to resolve. On failure, expect an error inside the
    detail.

### `/you/runs/[run_id]` (`you/(inbox)/runs/[run_id]/+page.ts`, `+page.svelte`)
- **Purpose:** a deep link to one run (push notifications point here, `relay-push-send.ts:159`).
- **Data:** segments, `listRuns` and `listGates`, **in the tenant taken from the `dojo_tenant`
  cookie** (`+page.ts:28,36-46`), not in the run's own dōjō.
- **Findings:**
  - **bug:** a member of two dōjōs who taps a push for a run in dōjō B while the cookie still
    holds A gets *"This run is not in your dōjōs."* (`+page.svelte:58-59`). The inbox layout
    usually covers this by finding the run in the fan-out (`(inbox)/+layout.svelte:30-31`), but
    this page's own load and its fallback text are wrong. A membership-less person (an empty
    cookie) gets `DojoJoinEmpty`.
  - **polish:** it loads the whole run list to find one run (`+page.ts:49`).
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a member of two dōjōs, enter dōjō A (sets the cookie), then open
    `/you/runs/<id of a run in dōjō B>` — expect that run. The code suggests the list finds it,
    but the page's own load would say "not in your dōjōs" if the fan-out has not loaded it.
  - [ ] Open `/console/relay/<id>` — expect a 308 to `/you/runs/<id>`.

### `/you/workspaces` (`you/[section]`, section `workspaces`)
- **Purpose:** every workspace you can read, across all your dōjōs.
- **Data:** `GET /v1/you/workspaces` → `dojo.workspaces` as the caller, decided by RLS
  (`can_read_workspace`: a role on one of its repositories, a viewer grant, or admin). An error
  shows `LoadError` (`ScrWorkspaces.svelte:42-43`). Correct.
- **Findings:**
  - **gap:** an empty list never says *why*. "Never synced", "sync failing" and "synced, no
    repositories" all read "No workspaces yet" (X5). This is exactly the production case.
  - **gap:** "Filter" does nothing (X3).
  - **bug (X2):** a contributor sees workspaces here but a 403 under `/org/{slug}/workspaces`.
- **Mockup:** `ScrProjects` (`dojo2-app.jsx:125`). The structure matches.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a contributor whose daemon synced, open `/you/workspaces` — expect your workspaces.
    Compare with `/org/{slug}/workspaces` for the same dōjō (expect the same; the code suggests a
    403 there).
  - [ ] As a dōjō viewer with one grant, open `/you/workspaces` — expect the granted workspace.

### `/you/workspaces/[id]` (`you/workspaces/[id]/+page.ts`)
- **Purpose:** a workspace's constitution preview (daemon-resolved ladder plus conflicts).
- **Data:** finds the workspace in `listUserWorkspaces`, then calls `loadWorkspaceConstitution`.
  The constitution error is shown (`+page.svelte:20-26`).
- **Findings:**
  - **bug:** `if (!hasMembership) redirect(...)` (`+page.ts:20`). A **dōjō viewer** has no
    membership by definition (D8), so the one workspace they were granted, listed on
    `/you/workspaces`, bounces straight back to the list when clicked. Viewers have no working
    drill-in at all. The invite-accept page sends them exactly here (`invites/accept/+page.ts:27`).
  - **gap (X4):** a read error turns into a silent redirect.
  - **gap:** the viewer's managerial view (metrics, dashboards, per-repository splits, D8) does
    not exist. The mockup has it (`dojo2-viewer.jsx`).
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a dōjō viewer granted workspace W, open `/you/workspaces` and click W — expect W's
    view. The code suggests you land back on the list.
  - [ ] As a contributor, open a workspace with no federated constitution — expect the "resolves
    in your editor" state, not an error.

- **Mockup:** `ScrConstitution` (`dojo2-app.jsx:139`) shows stance dials and a personal rung.

### `/you/packs` — Rule packs
- **Purpose:** browse the global pack library and adopt or drop packs.
- **Data:** `GET /v1/you/rule-packs` (library plus adopted). Writes `set_pack_adoption` (a
  definer function, caller from the session). Load and action errors are shown separately

- **Findings:**
  - **gap:** "Browse all" does nothing.
  - **question:** "Adopt" is personal, but packs are also adoptable at the dōjō, workspace and

- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Adopt a pack, reload — expect it still adopted. Drop it — expect it gone.
  - [ ] With the dōjō offline (bad network) — expect "Could not load the rule packs", not an

### `/you/sharing` — Sharing (repository elections)
- **Purpose:** decide, per repository, whether it is shared to the dōjō (`not_elected_user`
  repositories otherwise never sync).
- **Data:** `GET /v1/you/repositories` (`my_repositories`). Writes
  `PATCH /v1/you/repositories/election` → `set_repository_election` (definer; its refusal SQLSTATE
  keeps its HTTP status). Errors are kept separate from empty (`[section]/+page.ts:77-89`), and
  pending toggles are tracked.
- **Findings:**
  - **question:** `register_repository` checks dōjō membership, not forge access to the
    repository. Any member can register any key under a connected org, and names are
    first-writer-wins (`register_repository.ddl:87-93`). Do registered-but-not-mine repositories
    show up in Sharing?
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Toggle a repository on, reload — expect it still on. The next daemon sync should push its
    metrics.
  - [ ] Toggle when the dōjō refuses (an org-authority repository) — expect a reason, not a
    silent revert.

### `/you/dojos` — My dōjōs
- **Purpose:** your memberships grouped by kind, "Sync from GitHub" (re-provision from the
  forge), Azure DevOps connect and import, and a Microsoft-link notice.
- **Data:**
  - Memberships come from the layout.
  - `GET /v1/you/microsoft` gives the link status, and a failure is said, never read as "linked"
    (`[section]/+page.ts:104-113`).
  - Sync calls `POST /v1/you/provision`, which runs as the service role but takes the principal
    from the session and checks the forge token against the linked GitHub id

- **Findings:**
  - **gap:** member, workspace and pending counts are undefined (`dojo-orgs.ts:103-107`); the
    mockup (`ScrMyDojos`, `dojo2-app.jsx:1401`) shows them.
  - **question:** "Sync from GitHub" in the web app needs the GitHub token from the current
    session's `provider_token`. After the first request it only exists in the sealed
    `/v1/t`-scoped cookie (`hooks.server.ts:24-35`). Does `/v1/you/provision` see it, or does Sync
    always say "reconnect"?
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Click "Sync from GitHub" an hour or more after signing in — expect a clear message, either
    the result or "reconnect GitHub".
  - [ ] Join a new GitHub org, Sync — expect the new dōjō to appear.
  - [ ] Click a dōjō row — expect `/org/{id}`.

### `/you/contributions` — Contributions
- **Purpose:** what you shared, and downstream artifacts you can pin.
- **Data:** `GET /v1/you/contributions`. Writes `POST /v1/you/contributions/adopt`. Load and

  - [ ] Pin a downstream item — expect it to stick across a reload. With no network, expect an

### `/you/invites/accept?token=…` (`you/invites/accept/+page.ts`)
- **Purpose:** redeem an invite. A member is redirected into the dōjō; a viewer to
  `/you/workspaces`.
- **Data:** `POST /v1/you/invites/accept`, run as the service role (no membership exists yet). The
  email is matched in code (`invites-data.ts:142`). Rejections show their reason.
- **Findings:**
  - **question:** a **mutation inside a page `load`**. SvelteKit can run a load on link hover
    (preload) or again on client navigation. It is safe only if accept is idempotent per token,
    which `used` probably makes so; but a second run then shows "This invite couldn't be accepted
    — already used" to someone who did just join.
  - **bug (follows from `/you/workspaces/[id]`):** a viewer lands on Workspaces, and the workspace
    they were granted cannot be opened.
  - **polish:** a member redirect to `orgHref(result.tenant_id)` relies on the layout re-reading
    memberships after the accept. Otherwise `/org/[slug]` redirects to `/you` as "not a member".
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As an invited member (matching email), open the link — expect to land in the dōjō home,
    not bounce to `/you`.
  - [ ] Open the same link again — expect a clear "already used" message, and you are still a
    member.
  - [ ] Open it signed in as a different email — expect "wrong email".
  - [ ] As an invited viewer, open the link and then the granted workspace — expect its view (the
    code bounces you).

## Org zone (`/org/[slug]/...`)

The slug is the tenant **id**. Every load resolves the org against *your* memberships and
redirects to `/you` if you are not a member (`org/[slug]/+page.ts:22-23`, `[section]/+page.ts:94-96`).
That part is correct. All data goes through `/v1/t/{tenant key}/...` with your JWT.

### `/org/[slug]` — Org home
- **Purpose:** the dōjō's workspaces, a "Needs a maintainer" band (open triage) and a stat row.
- **Data:** `GET .../workspaces` (lead floor, X2) and, from maintainer, `GET .../triage`. The
  triage error is shown (`ScrOrgHome.svelte:78-79`); **the workspaces error is dropped (X1)**.
- **Findings:**
  - **bug (X1+X2):** a contributor or maintainer always sees "No workspaces in this jurisdiction
    yet … Nothing is hidden", and the header reads "0 workspaces under this dōjō".
  - **gap:** the members and "need a maintainer" stats are hidden because the counts are
    undefined (`dojo-orgs.ts:103-107`; `ScrOrgHome.svelte:61-72`). Only "workspaces · in flight"
    shows, and it counts all workspaces, not ones in flight. The mockup shows all three
    (`dojo2-app.jsx:632-650`).
  - **gap:** band actions do nothing — `onAct` is not passed (`org/[slug]/+page.svelte:22-29`);
    "By team" does nothing.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As an **admin**, open the org home — expect the workspaces the daemon filed.
  - [ ] As a **contributor** with a role on a repository in workspace W — expect W listed. The
    code suggests "No workspaces … Nothing is hidden".
  - [ ] As a maintainer with open triage — expect the band to list it. Click an action in the
    band — expect something to happen (the code: nothing).
  - [ ] Visit `/org/<an id you are not in>` — expect a redirect to `/you`.

  - **gap:** the include toggles are kept in memory only and are lost on reload. "Adopt pack" does

- **Mockup:** `ScrOrgLadder` (`dojo2-app.jsx:719`). The layout matches; the mockup's editor is
  also local.
- **Checklist:**

    crash or blank pane.
  - [ ] Write a rule, Save, reload — expect it published (as a maintainer+). The code: it is gone,

  - [ ] As a contributor, check whether "New rule" shows at all — expect hidden (POST
    `.../rules` needs maintainer).

### `/org/[slug]/teams` — Teams
- **Purpose:** team totals for every member; per-person rows for a lead (their own teams) and an
  admin (D38).
- **Data:** `GET .../team-metrics` (member floor) → `team_metric_totals` (definer, k-anonymity ≥ 3

    identities are "R own".
  - **mockup:** `ScrTeams` (`dojo2-app.jsx:1687`) has Teams / People / Inflow tabs, a stat row
    (first-try %, sessions, in triage) and the toggles for "what a local sensei sends". The app
    shows only the metrics view. **gap.**
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As a contributor on a team of 3 or fewer others — expect totals hidden (k-anonymity).
    With 4 or more — expect totals, never per person.
  - [ ] As a lead on a team — expect per-person rows only for teammates, and only on
    repositories you can read.
  - [ ] As a lead on no team — expect only yourself.

### `/org/[slug]/members` and `/org/[slug]/audit` — Role surfaces (admin)
- **Purpose:** one screen with Members · Viewers · Teams · Policies · Audit tabs.
- **Data:** six parallel reads (members, policies, audit, viewers, workspaces, teams) in **one**
  `Promise.all` (`[section]/+page.ts:154-175`). Opening the Audit tab fetches all six.
- **Interactions:**
  - set a role (`PATCH .../members/{id}/role`, admin floor, audited);
  - invite a member or viewer (`POST .../invites`, rate-limited);
  - grant or revoke a viewer's workspace, remove a viewer;
  - create, rename or delete a team, add or remove a team member, grant a team's view.
  - Failures land in `org.actionError` as a dismissible toast (`[section]/+page.svelte:204-221`),
    which is good.
- **Findings:**
  - **bug (X6):** the "you" chip never shows, and the admin can demote themselves or the last
    admin.
  - **bug:** if **any** of the six reads fails (for example `viewers` or `teams`), members,
    policies and audit **all** go empty, with one shared error.
  - **bug:** member rows are keyed by `m.name` (`ScrRoleSurfaces.svelte:158`). Two people with
    the same display name, or two unnamed members whose `shortId` collides, raise Svelte's
    duplicate-key error.
  - **gap:** the Policies tab ignores `dojo.policies`. `toKitRolePolicies` returns a constant
    ladder and drops its argument (`admin-map.ts:98-100`), yet the page still fetches the policies.
    The text for "lead" ("everything a maintainer can") no longer matches D38 (team lead reads
    teammates' metrics).
  - **gap:** the audit trail **never shows who did it**. Every entry is voiced as `sensei`
    (`admin-map.ts:115-121`); `actor_id` is dropped. "Export" does nothing.
  - **question:** a failed `recordAudit` is only logged (`audit.ts:43-46`), so a role change can
    succeed with no audit row.
- **Mockup:** `ScrRoleSurfaces` (`dojo2-app.jsx:830`) and `ScrAudit` (:1790). Tabs match; the
  mockup's audit names actors.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] As an admin, open Members — expect a "you" chip and no role picker on your own row. The
    code: a picker.
  - [ ] Change your own role to contributor — expect a refusal (last admin). The code: it
    succeeds, and you lose the Admin nav.
  - [ ] Change another member's role — expect it to stick, plus an audit entry naming you.
  - [ ] Open Audit — expect "who · what · when". The code shows "sensei" as the speaker for every
    entry.
  - [ ] Invite a viewer, then grant them a workspace — expect it under Viewers (and see the viewer
    drill-in bug).
  - [ ] Create a team, add a member, rename it, delete it — each should show, and a failure should
    toast.

## `/v1` routes the daemon calls (sync)

All `/v1/you/*` routes resolve the caller from a Bearer token or the session cookie, fail closed
(`dojo-auth.ts:88-132`), and write through `callerDb` (the caller's JWT, RLS). Database errors
are 5xx, never an empty 200 — except as noted below.

| Route | Gate / function | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /v1/you/repositories` (register_repository) | `register_repository` (definer, `is_member`), then `file_repository_workspace` per repository | Idempotent (ON CONFLICT). Up to 1000 repositories. **One bad key makes the whole batch a 500** after the earlier repositories are already committed (`repositories.ts:174`), so registration, plan and metrics are blocked for every repository on every cycle (**bug, latent**). `workspace_error` comes back inside a 200 and only reaches a daemon warn log. |
| `GET /v1/you/sync/plan` | `my_repositories`, `metric_activations`, `my_graph_branches` | Pages past the 1000-row cap. 5xx on error. |
| `PATCH /v1/you/repositories/election`, `PATCH /v1/you/metrics/activation` | definer functions | The refusal SQLSTATE keeps its status. |
| `POST/GET /v1/you/metrics` | `file_metric_rows` (the principal comes from the session) | 2 MB / 1000 rows. No existence oracle. |
| `POST/GET /v1/you/graph` | graph ingest | 4 MB. 404/403/409. |
| `GET /v1/you/memberships`, `/preferences`, `/workspaces`, `/contributions` | caller's JWT | 500 on error. |
| `POST /v1/you/provision` | service role, principal from the session, forge token checked | Listed exception. |
| `/v1/auth/cli/token`, `refresh`, `whoami` | Supabase passthrough | **No rate limit** on any `/v1/you/*` or CLI-token route (only invites and Azure use `rate-limit.ts`). |

Findings to try:
- **bug:** a Keychain or token failure is invisible on the dōjō and shows as green in the desktop
  app's Settings (X5).
- **gap/security:** `file_repository_workspace` takes a caller-supplied `p_repository_id`, checks
  only `is_member`, and can be called directly through PostgREST. It is missing from the matrix's
  definer list.
- **bug (RLS review):** `attach_run_to_workspace` checks only `is_member`, so any member can
  overwrite **any** workspace's `constitution` in the tenant, including ones they cannot read.
- **question:** `register_repository`'s `no_connection` vs `not_a_member` tells any signed-in
  stranger whether some dōjō has connected org X.
- **question:** a 500-repository registration makes 1–2 RPCs per repository in one Worker
  request. Check this against the Cloudflare subrequest limit.
- **polish:** raw Postgres `error.message` is returned in 500 bodies (`repositories.ts:174`,
  `metrics-ingest.ts:100,112`). `parseRepos` silently drops empty keys despite its "malformed is
  a 400" comment.

Checklist (with the daemon):
- [ ] Revoke the daemon's Keychain approval, wait for a sync tick. Expect the desktop Settings ›
  dōjō row to say "failing". The code: it stays green; only Activity › Scheduled tasks shows it.
- [ ] Sync with one repository whose remote is malformed (for example
  `ssh.dev.azure.com/org/proj/repo` without `v3`). Expect the others to still register. The code
  suggests the whole batch 500s every cycle.
- [ ] After a good sync, confirm the dōjō's org home lists the workspaces **as a contributor**
  (X1/X2).

## Top issues, ranked

1. **The org home shows "No workspaces … Nothing is hidden" whenever the read fails, and it fails
   for every contributor and maintainer** (X1 + X2). `org/[slug]/+page.svelte:22-29` drops
   `workspacesError`, and `GET .../workspaces` has a lead floor. This is the screen the production
   report was about, and it actively tells the user nothing is wrong. — **bug**
2. **Sync failure is invisible end to end** (X5). The daemon's token failure writes no
   `sync_state`, the desktop app stays green, the dōjō keeps no heartbeat, and three different
   states render identically. — **bug/gap**
3. **The last admin can be demoted; the self-guard is broken** by comparing a principal id with a
   Supabase auth id (X6). There is no DB or route guard. — **bug**
4. **Org Constitution "New rule" discards what you wrote, and crashes on an empty dōjō**
   (`ScrOrgLadder.svelte:42-46`, `org-ladder-state.svelte.ts:61`). It is shown to every role. —
   **bug**
5. **Any member can rewrite workspaces they cannot read.** `attach_run_to_workspace` (overwrites
   the constitution) and `file_repository_workspace` (refile or rename, undo an admin's mapping)
   gate on `is_member` only, and both are callable directly through PostgREST. — **bug/security**
6. **Viewers cannot open the workspace they were granted.** `/you/workspaces/[id]` redirects
   anyone without a membership, which by D8 is every viewer. Invite-accept sends them straight
   there. — **bug**
7. **The triage detail shows the wrong candidate's evidence, and no decision can be made**
   (Approve, Revise, Decline, and Approvals' Review/Approve, are not wired, although the route
   exists). — **bug + gap**
8. **Seat binding looks up a namespace across tenants and swallows seat-write errors**
   (`relay/session/+server.ts:96-107`). Money-facing. — **bug**
9. **One bad repository key blocks the whole sync on every cycle**
   (`repositories.ts:174`, a 500 after a partial commit). — **bug (latent)**
10. **The members screen goes blank on any one of six reads failing; the audit has no actor; the
    Policies tab is a constant.** Contributors receive teammates' emails through `GET .../teams`.
    — **bug + gap**
11. **Twenty-odd buttons do nothing** (X3), the self-host block contradicts D17, and the Needs
    bell is fixed at 0. — **gap/polish**
12. **The `/you/runs/[id]` load uses the cookie's dōjō, not the run's**, and the stale-cookie and
    fixture tenant fallback (X7) is still live. — **bug/question**

**Testing note:** every local membership is `admin`. Items 1, 5, 6 and the Teams, Knowledge and
triage role cells cannot be reproduced locally until a contributor, a maintainer, a lead and a
viewer are seeded.

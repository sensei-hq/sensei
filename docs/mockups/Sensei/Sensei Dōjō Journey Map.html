01

Overview

要

Dōjō was specified as the one structural gap the individual-developer product couldn't reach: a private, company-hosted hive-mind sitting between the lone developer and the global Collective. This map walks both sides of it — the developer in the Observatory, and the three governing roles in the console — and carries each moment's resolution forward into a status ledger. Friction and gaps were anticipated during ideation; the resolutions are the calls that became the brief, and every journey-stage resolution is now realised as a screen — only server infrastructure and identity plumbing remain.

決
From ideation to build

Every journey stage is built — Relay, governance-authoring and personal Dōjōs now join them.

The design pass turned every original call into a screen — triage, candidate review with a second-approver gate and a “who gets this” dry-run, scopes & policies, members, clients, audit, admin Monitor; join, connect, bind, share, travel and downstream in the app. This revision adds more: Relay in the Dōjō (watch · approve · decide · nudge · chat, on phone and in the console, reaching a live session through the Dōjō — no pairing), a Developer console (your teams, contributions and what's distributed to you across every Dōjō), Governance authoring (define the stance, rules, skills, agents, commands and a project's memory per scope, so a new developer inherits it on join) — down to a personal Dōjō where an individual sets their own constitution for their own projects, across all their linked emails. This revision also makes entry ungated — a signed-in developer with no Dōjō works fully solo on their own projects and running tasks, and Relay is never gated by the plan — adds an effective-constitution preview that resolves the Company · Client · Personal · Project · Stack ladder for a project with conflicts settled before the first commit, and a constitution library that seeds governance from proven packs (principles, patterns, HIPAA / PCI / SOC 2 / GDPR, stack reviewers wiring OSS checkers, design-system guards) — prevention over cure. What stays open is infrastructure (server stand-up, SSO / SCIM) — and the business model, newly set out in §5.

02

System architecture

構

How the pieces fit. Everything a developer touches runs locally on their device — the desktop app, a daemon, its own Postgres and Ollama, the CLI and an MCP service — private by default. The Dōjō is the only shared tier: a managed SaaS on Supabase, with optional self-hosted servers that register back to it. GitHub is the spine of identity and discovery; orgs without GitHub use a magic link.

03

In-app journey — the developer

連

The contributor's path inside the Observatory: discover an org, authenticate, bind a project, preview what governs it, let a rule, insight or memory form, share it upstream, watch it travel, and receive governed knowledge back. The Dōjō is a two-way receiver — it consolidates what individuals and teams contribute, a maintainer approves, and the lesson is promoted across the ladder and fanned back out to everyone in scope. Each stage reads through the five lenses, then the committed resolution and its build status.

04

Console & Relay — roles and away-from-keyboard

基

The company-hosted site, shared by four roles: maintainers govern what gets taught, admins run the server and author the governance model, client / engagement leads guard confidentiality, and every developer gets a personal view across all their Dōjōs. Then Relay — the away-from-keyboard surface, folded into the Dōjō on phone and console. Each is its own track.

05

Business model

商

The line is drawn by who the knowledge is for, not by features. A public / open-source Dōjō and your personal solo Dōjō are free forever — full governance, full Relay. Payment begins only when a Dōjō is private and shared by a team or org. The desktop app, the global Collective, and inference (bring-your-own-key) are always free — there are no tokens to mark up.

商
The one line

Free where public or personal. Paid where private and shared.

Anyone can create a Dōjō for free — for an open-source project, a community, or just themselves. The moment a Dōjō becomes a private, multi-member space — a company or team coordinating knowledge it doesn't want to publish — it moves to a paid tier. You're paying for the coordination and governance layer over a group's private knowledge, plus the control and compliance a business needs (self-hosting, SSO, audit, all included) — not for the product itself.

公Public / OSS
Free · forever
Open-source projects & communities. Public, opt-in knowledge; unlimited members; github/<org>. Full governance authoring + Relay for your own projects.
己Personal
Free
Your own constitution & rules for your own projects. Private to you, across every linked email; personal/<you>. Solo & private is still free — Relay included.
組Teampaid
Per seat / mo
Private shared Dōjō for a company or team: private scopes, role consoles, governance authoring, shared-team Relay (inbox, presence, priority realtime), client engagements. Billed per active contributor — read-only is free. Self-hosting available — same per-seat price.
携
Relay is free for individuals — paid only where it's shared

The core loop — watch · approve · decide · nudge · chat on your own projects — is free on the personal / OSS tier (fair use: one active machine, standard realtime, native-app push). The paid tiers add the coordination around it, where the real infra cost lives: a shared team inbox & queue, presence, higher concurrency, priority realtime and an approval-audit trail (Team); self-hosted relay, SSO on mobile and long-term approval-trail retention. Metering follows cost — concurrency and team size, not the individual — so Relay stays a hook, not a paywall.

種

Public seeds the network

Free public & personal Dōjōs are the top of the funnel: they grow the global Collective and get sensei into every developer's own workflow. Teams convert when private, shared knowledge becomes worth coordinating.

蔵

Pay for control, not tokens

Inference is bring-your-own-key and runs locally — there's no token cost to resell. Revenue is the coordination layer: private scopes, governance, distribution, and the compliance surface (SSO, audit, self-host).

信

Confidentiality is the wedge

Client anonymization, the immutable audit trail, and self-hosting are what regulated and agency businesses buy — included in the paid team plan at the same per-seat price. That's exactly where a private hive-mind earns its keep.

問

Open pricing questions

Still unsettled: the exact seat definition (active vs read-only), whether self-hosting is a license or a subscription, fair-use limits on free public Dōjōs, storage caps on personal Dōjōs, and non-profit / education pricing.
